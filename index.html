Year Rollover

PIN-protected · Resets all usage and starts a new policy year

🔐
Administrator Access Required
Enter the 4-digit PIN to access Year Rollover controls
⚠️ Year Rollover — Read carefully before proceeding

This function will perform the following actions on your Google Sheet:

  • Archive the current FF_Master_Log by renaming it with the old policy year
  • Create a fresh FF_Master_Log tab ready for the new year
  • Reset all employee used values (Tickets Used, Food Used, Nights Used) to zero in FF_Summary
  • Update the Policy Year label across the system
  • Apply the uploaded staff list: add new, reactivate returning, deactivate leavers
  • All employee limits are set from the uploaded tier values
  • No employee records, historical submissions or void entries are deleted

After the rollover, submissions will continue immediately on the new policy year with zero used amounts.
